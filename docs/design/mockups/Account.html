Account and backup

Your progress is saved on this device. Sign in to keep it in sync everywhere.

Sign in to sync

You can use the guide without an account. Signing in saves your progress online and combines it with what is already on this device.

Backup file

Download your progress as a file, or add a backup you saved earlier. It works with or without an account.

Adding a backup keeps everything you have and adds what the file has. It never removes progress.

Start a new run

Clears your progress on this device so you can start again from nothing. You get a chance to download a backup first.

How saving works

  • On this device

    Every change is saved here first, so the guide works without an account.

  • In your account

    After you sign in, your progress syncs between your devices.

  • When they differ

    The first time you sign in, both are combined. After that, the most recent change wins.
