Gate notes:

- **Act 2 starts at the Last Judge** in the northeast of the Blasted Steps: ring five Bellshrines (The Marrow, Deep Docks, Greymoor, Bellhart, Shellwood), then play the Needolin at the great door [wiki (snippet), RR (Rogue Ranker, Last Judge page), DG says six bells: conflict, five has more support]. Beating her opens the Grand Gate [several]. The other way in is the Phantom in Bilewater's Exhaust Organ, whose lift also leads up [wiki (snippet), SK]; the Route uses the Last Judge.
- **Grand Gate, Underworks, Choral Chambers:** inside the Grand Gate a lift crashes down into the Underworks, and the climb out leads to the Choral Chambers; a breakable wall high in the Grand Gate also leads there [wiki (snippet), SK, GPM low]. All three need only the Last Judge.
- **Cogwork Core:** GC counts the Cogwork Dancers as its boss; one source puts them at the top of the Choral Chambers, and beating them opens floor grates into the Cogwork Core [SK, IIG]. The Region is gated on the Last Judge, and everything past the Dancers requires them.
- **Whispering Vaults:** entered from the southeast of the Cogwork Core [IIG]; gated on the Dancers.
- **Whiteward:** a lift in the southwest of the Choral Chambers, opened with the White Key [wiki (snippet), DG, GBT].
- **High Halls:** above the top left of the Choral Chambers; the way needs the Clawline [wiki (snippet), TA]. One source says after the Whispering Vaults [NC]: not used.
- **The Slab:** a long bridge from the southwest of the Choral Chambers, available once in the Citadel [GG]. Being captured is another way in that strips most equipment [GG]; not modelled.
- **Mount Fay:** past The Slab, the climb needs the Clawline [several].
- **Memorium:** a platform in the top right of the Choral Chambers that needs the Faydown Cloak [DG, one source].
- **Putrified Ducts:** from the Memorium; the Faydown Cloak is required [DG, AT, SK]; the wiki (snippet) adds a second way in from the top of Bilewater.
- **Wisp Thicket:** the Faydown Cloak is required [DG, SK, gamerblurb low].
- **Bilewater:** reachable in Act 1 from Sinner's Road. Conflict on the ability: Swift Step [PCG], Cling Grip [SHK], Cling Grip and Drifter's Cloak [one more]. Cling Grip is the latest of these and already implies the other two through Act 1's chain. The second way in, from the Songclave, needs the Faydown Cloak and Clawline [PCG].
- **Sands of Karak:** Clawline is required [AT, wiki (snippet)]; the wiki (snippet) adds the Faydown Cloak in Act 2 (Silk Soar in Act 3). Both are used: Locked a little longer is safer.
- **The Cradle:** reached by playing the complete Threefold Melody in the Cogwork Dancers' room [several].
