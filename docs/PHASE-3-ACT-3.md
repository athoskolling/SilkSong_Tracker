## Interim notes (research in progress; folded into the sections below when done)

- Act 3 starts after the Silk and Soul wish (Caretaker, Songclave): bring Maiden's Soul (Bone Bottom), Hermit's Soul (below the Bellhart Bellway), Seeker's Soul (Bilewater) and the Snare Setter (Weavenest Atla) back, then beat Grand Mother Silk and use the Needolin instead of Bind. [DG-A3, NL-A3, PCG-A3, DST, II-A3, wiki (snippet)]
- Act 3 chain: After the Fall, then Spell Seeker (Snail Shamans teach the Elegy of the Deep), then The Old Hearts (Khann: Encrusted Heart, Coral Tower, Sands of Karak; Nyleth: Pollen Heart, beyond Shrine Guardian Seth in the Grand Gate; Karmelita: Hunter's Heart, northeast Far Fields via Hunter's March), Red Memory, Everbloom, then Lost Lace in the Abyss. Clover Dancers (Lost Verdania, Craw Lake, Greymoor) give a Conjoined Heart that can stand in for one heart.
- Silk Soar: Weavenest Absolom in the Abyss behind a Needolin door [SK-SOAR, AT-SOAR]; Nintendo Life says Weavenest Atla (likely mislabelled).
