# Moss Grotto: candidate list for Athos (orchestrator, 2026-10-20)

Athos asked for this (option 1 after item 4: guides give a **starting list**, Athos confirms or strikes each line from his own play and the main wiki, then writes the "how" in his own words). **Nothing here is data.** It holds names, kinds and places only; no text was copied. Read once each, on 2026-10-20: [game-checklists.com, Moss Grotto](https://game-checklists.com/silksong/moss-grotto.html) (GC), [Game8, Moss Grotto 100% map](https://game8.co/games/Hollow-Knight-Silksong/archives/548810) (G8), [TheGamer, Moss Grotto, Weavenest Atla and Bone Bottom](https://www.thegamer.com/hollow-knight-silksong-moss-grotto-weavenest-atla-bone-bottom-collectibles-walkthrough/) (TG). hollowknight.wiki and Fextralife were not opened.

"Agree" counts the sources that name the line the same way. Types use the approved list (`docs/CONTRACTS.md` rule 12).

## Questions to settle first (they change the list)

1. **Weavenest Atla**: a Sub-area of Moss Grotto, or its own Region? TG groups it with Moss Grotto, but says it opens only after a boss in Bellhart. If it is its own Region, lines C8, C10, C11 and T4 move there (a later batch).
2. **Bonegrave / Chapel of the Wanderer** (west of Moss Grotto): a Sub-area of Moss Grotto? GC and G8 list the Wanderer Crest under Moss Grotto.
3. **Late items**: some lines need abilities from much later (Cling Grip, Silk Soar, Needolin) or Act 3. They stay in Moss Grotto with a late `step` (the Route is global), unless you prefer to leave Act 3 lines for a later batch.
